egod (15): the portal's only password field could not be read back, and its one rule was never stated

/egod/login is the whole of system 15's auth -- the same form is both sign-in
and sign-up -- and its password field was a bare masked input. §1א asks for the
eye on every password field and for the password rules to be shown beside the
field while typing; neither was there.

The 6 is measured, not assumed: a signup POST to the project's auth endpoint
with a 4-character password answers 422 weak_password, "Password should be at
least 6 characters". PASSWORD_MIN_LENGTH now feeds the label, the minLength and
the submit guard, so the three cannot drift apart. The rule renders only in
signup mode -- an existing password has no rule to meet -- and turns gold with a
check at 6.

components/ui/password-input.tsx mirrors torah's: type, aria-pressed, aria-label
and icon all derive from one state, so the button cannot say "show" while the
password is already visible. type="button" is explicit -- the field sits in a
<form>, and a button with no type submits. The toggle sits on the LEFT because
the page is RTL and the right side already carries the Lock icon.

That Lock is also the trap torah hit: PasswordInput's relative wrapper comes
after the absolutely-positioned icon in the DOM, so at z-index auto the wrapper
paints over it. z-10 on the icon, verified with elementFromPoint rather than by
eye, against the untouched Mail icon as the control.

Deployed to production and verified on the domain: 3 characters now stops at the
browser's own Hebrew length message with zero auth requests, and no test user was
created. The live bundle hash matched neither the _deploy copy nor a fresh build,
so before overwriting it the live JS was downloaded and its strings compared --
nothing existed live that the new build lacks.
