21 mthbram: round-3 dark recheck - footer copyright text was invisible (theme-independent, not dark-specific)

contrast-probe.mjs found 4 identical failures across light/dark and both widths (mthbram is
dark-by-design, no toggle - identical-across-schemes confirms this isn't a dark-mode regression).
2 are the known gradient bg-clip-text tool-limitation already documented for torah/modaot/smachot/egod.
The 3rd/real one: Footer.tsx copyright line used text-primary-foreground/40, and this app's
--primary-foreground token is the same dark navy as the footer background (button-text color, not a
muted-text token) - at 40% opacity the text composited to ~1:1 contrast against its own background,
effectively invisible on every load regardless of theme. Fixed to text-gold-cream/70, matching the
sibling elements in the same footer.

Deploy note: apps/21-mthbram's Vercel project (mthbram-more30) has no vercel.json and is configured
Framework=Other / Build=echo no-build / Output=. - it expects a pre-built upload with the vite dist
output nested under a physical mthbram/ folder (vite base is /mthbram/, and the portal rewrite proxies
to mthbram-more30.vercel.app/mthbram/:path*). A plain `vercel --prod` from apps/21-mthbram uploads the
raw source as-is under those settings and does not rebuild - this briefly 404'd more30.com/mthbram/
before being caught and fixed by staging dist/* under a mthbram/ subfolder with an SPA-fallback
vercel.json and deploying that instead. Verified live: index/asset/SPA-route all 200, and the contrast
probe is clean of the real failure in all four scheme/width combinations. Evidence:
QA/platform/mthbram-dark-recheck-0818/_results.md.
