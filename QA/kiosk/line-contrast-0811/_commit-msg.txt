kiosk (35): one dialog, two clickable rows, two different borders

Item 4 of STATUS.md "Next, in order" — the console-wide --line question that
three contrast steps each recorded and deliberately left. Items 1-3 are still
blocked: the deploy builds from l023131500-ops/zol, and both Android items need
a toolchain this checkout does not have.

Each of those steps split something out of --line into its own token and left
the token itself open at 1.29:1 dark / ~1.2:1 light. A fourth guessed token was
the wrong move, so this measured every --line consumer that renders in the
console, both colour schemes, against the surface actually painted behind it.

They split into 1.4.11's two kinds and the split is not close. One control:
.wz-step is a <label> wrapping a checkbox with the whole row clickable, at
1.22:1 light and 1.29:1 dark against the card -- and 1.16:1 / 1.37:1 against the
sunken fill a *ticked* row takes, so the row a person had already acted on had
the weakest edge of the three. What makes that a mistake rather than a choice is
two lines up in the same dialog: .wz-track label is the identical shape, a label
wrapping a radio, and was already on --field-border at 3.30 / 3.42.

So the fix is that one declaration and no new token. Now 3.30 / 3.13 light,
3.42 / 3.64 dark. .wz-step.wz-next still overrides with --accent and was
re-measured (5.28 / 3.22) so this is not claimed to have fixed it.

Everything else is a separator and 1.4.11 exempts separators: card edge 1.14:1
light / 1.42:1 dark, and the card's own fill against the page is 1.07 / 1.10.
None is the only thing marking its boundary -- cards carry --shadow and a fill,
.hl-row and .wz-step.wz-ticked carry --sunken. Raising --line to 3:1 would put a
mid-grey rule around every card, stat tile and table row on every screen: a
redesign of the console, not an accessibility fix. Left deliberately, but
printed as info rows in the run rather than left silent, so the cost is a number
for whoever revisits it.
