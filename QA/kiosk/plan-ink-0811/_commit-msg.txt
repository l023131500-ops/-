kiosk (35): the tick beside every price was a colour no sweep could read

apps/35-kioskfleet/STATUS.md item 6 ended on the one thing admin-contrast-0811
found and deliberately left: "--accent-2 is text in one more place,
.plan li::before on the marketing page, which is a different screen and
untouched here." That is this step, and it is the same defect with a harder bar.

The ✓ before every line of every pricing card was 2.28:1 in light — the identical
number the admin screen produced, because it is the identical #22c55e on the
identical white --card. What differs is the threshold. .stat .v is 32px/800 and
got 1.4.3's large-text carve-out at 3:1; the ✓ is font-weight 800 at the body's
16px, and 800 only becomes large text from 18.66px, so it is graded at 4.5:1.
The value misses by a factor of 1.97 rather than 1.32.

Fifth appearance of the shape a brand token beside a surface that inverts, and
second in a row where a stylesheet sweep could not have looked. Last time it was
an inline style= inside a JS template. This time it is generated content, and the
reason is worth more than this screen: getComputedStyle(el) does not return a
pseudo-element's colour, so every contrast harness written under item 6 has been
blind to ::before and ::after on every page it ever swept — not only this one.
plan-ink-0811's PAINTED takes a pseudo argument and is the first that is not.

Fixed as --ok-ink, the token the previous step created for exactly this: #15803d
light (5.02:1, measured), #22c55e dark. The bug was light-mode only, so the dark
page is byte-identical — 7.47:1 before and after. --accent-2 stays the brand's
green and stays right as a surface: .brand .logo's gradient and .dot.on do not
move. After this, no use of --accent-2 as text remains anywhere in
server/public/.

24/24 measured rows, exit 0. Twelve per mode: the ✓ on the first card, on the
featured one and on the last item of the third, plus the nine things painted
beside them on those cards — section heading, two plan names, two prices, the
/חודש, the item text itself and both buttons — so a fix cannot pass by moving
the neighbours out from under the probe. None of the nine was ever under
threshold. Four control rows, three of which must fail and do: an injected bad
value per mode (one grey cannot be a near-miss on both a white card and a
near-black one), and a "before" row that re-measures #22c55e instead of quoting
it — failing in light and passing in dark, which is the defect's own shape and
what stops the dark row reading as a second bug. Two further fact rows: that the
::before generated "✓" at all (a pseudo that never rendered and one with a
correct colour look identical through a ratio), and that the <head> script did
switch :root.dark on — it is a matchMedia toggle, not a media query in the
stylesheet, and without it both passes would have graded the light page.

Found and not fixed: the dashed --line between list items is 1.22:1 light /
1.29:1 dark against the card, reported and not asserted — the console-wide --line
question every contrast step has left open on purpose, and a separator between
two pricing rows is not where it gets decided.
