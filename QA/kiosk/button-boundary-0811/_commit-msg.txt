kiosk (35): a button that is a pale fill on a pale card has no shape, only text

.btn-light is #eef3ff on a white card and .btn-danger is #fee2e2 on the same:
1.11:1 and 1.22:1, and 1.04:1 where the button sits on the page rather than on
a card. WCAG 1.4.11 wants 3:1 from the boundary that identifies a control, and
here there was none — the only thing marking these as pressable was the label
inside them. That is every cancel, every edit and every delete in the console.
It is item 4 of STATUS.md's "Next, in order", left explicitly by the previous
contrast step.

Two tokens, --btn-light-edge and --btn-danger-edge, drawn as an inset ring:

- one value each rather than a light/dark pair. These two fills are the "light
  chips stay light" decision from the previous step, so they do not invert;
  each ring is therefore chosen against every surface such a button lands on —
  white card, dark card, --navy, the page background in both modes, and the
  button's own fill from the inside. Worst case 3.79 and 3.32.
- the button's ink could not be reused, which was the obvious move: #1f4fd8 is
  2.57:1 and #b91c1c is 2.63:1 against the dark card. Both were picked against
  a light fill and only ever sit on one.
- box-shadow: inset, not border. .btn declares border: none, so a real border
  grows every button by 2px, and .btn-sm buttons pack into a row inside a device
  card — exactly where QA/kiosk/clients-console-0811 already found a column of
  buttons running off the card's edge. An inset ring takes no space and follows
  the border-radius exactly.

Verified in QA/kiosk/button-boundary-0811: a real Chromium at both colorScheme
values against a stub serving the real server/public/, every number read from
getComputedStyle, and the background taken from the surface actually painted
behind each button rather than from the token — these sit on .modal, .device,
.card and the page itself, so one quoted value would be right for some and wrong
for the rest. 24/26, the two failures being the "before" rows, which is what
shows the check can fail. 70/71 on node --test, the documented baseline
(routing.test.mjs still imports express).

Two corrections the run forced, so the claim is no larger than the change: the
defect was light-mode only, because the same pale fills on a dark card were
already their own boundary at 13.9:1 and 16.9:1; and .btn-primary was first
measured on the card's edit button, which is itself a .btn-light. Read from the
real primary it is 5.28 / 3.22 and needed nothing.

Found and not fixed, so it is not silently claimed: a box-shadow is not painted
in Windows high-contrast mode. Every one of these is a real <button>, so the UA
supplies ButtonBorder there — enough in practice, but not measured here.

Not deployed; the Railway service still serves the previous console. The app
source is gitignored, so the change itself is recorded in STATUS.md.
