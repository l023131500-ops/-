kiosk (35): the page held in the installer's hand had a button with no edge

install.html was the second of the three public/ pages never measured for
contrast, and the one read by whoever is standing at the tablet: it carries the
two values EnrollActivity asks for and five steps followed literally.

The two .copy buttons are the only controls on it. A .btn fill elsewhere in this
fleet is its own boundary; here the button declares a real border, and its fill
is rgba(255,255,255,.12) over the .value box - 1.2:1, no boundary at all. So
that one line is the whole of what says "pressable", and at .34 it was 2.65:1
against the fill inside it. Now .45: 3.51:1 inside, 5.13:1 against the box
outside.

:hover made the same edge fainter - it lightened the fill and left the border
alone, 2.36:1, below even the resting value. Hovering a control must not weaken
it, and 1.4.11 covers states, so .copy:hover now carries a border-color of its
own at .58 (3.90:1).

The defect was one-sided, and the harness's first version wrongly demanded the
"before" row fail against the .value box too. It did not: 3.87:1. The edge was
sufficient outside and vanished inside. That row is kept and annotated rather
than dropped - the shape of the bug is part of the result.

A difference from the launcher page that the harness's own guard caught: there,
only the placeholder and the borders were translucent, and check() throws on any
non-opaque colour reaching lum(). Here the text is translucent too - .muted and
table.errors th are rgba(255,255,255,.72), which is most of the prose - so the
first run died on #lede rather than reporting a flattering 8.6:1. Every
foreground now goes through the same composite a border does.

QA/kiosk/install-contrast-0811 - a real Chromium at both colorScheme values,
backdrop sampled from the pixels Chromium painted (nothing on this page is
opaque), both depths driven, against install-link-0811/stub-server.mjs reused
rather than copied. 20/22 graded rows pass; the two failures are the before
rows, re-injected into the same DOM in the same run. Two full-page screenshots.
152 tests, 151 pass - the documented baseline, unchanged, since this step adds
no server code.

Found and not fixed, with the number rather than silence: the step-number disc
is 2.01:1 against the card. The digit on it carries the number at 5.28:1, and
the disc is --accent, the brand colour every page here uses identically - so
moving it is more30-priority.md section 6's design work, not a contrast token.
The .card, .value, .expect and .warn borders are separators and message frames,
which 1.4.11 exempts.

install.html itself is under apps/, which is gitignored here; the change is
recorded in apps/35-kioskfleet/STATUS.md. Not deployed.
