smel (12): the city the customer typed now decides which address the registry returns

core.issues #158, the half the banner could not cover. The 12/08 step shipped a
warning that says: you asked about Jerusalem and got Tel Aviv. This is the
matching itself.

Reproduced at the root against the real govmap endpoint, not a fixture, and the
cause was not the one the symptom suggested. The plain query «יפו 30 ירושלים»
already returned the correct Jerusalem address — one result, id
address|ADDR|53576669, with a valid POINT shape. It was thrown away because that
is the SHORT id form and carries no street field, so the exact-street test read
an empty string. geocodeAddress then ran its שדרות/רחוב/דרך prefix variations,
one of which surfaced «דרך יפו-ת״א 30» in TELAVIVYAFO — a long id whose street
field contains the token «יפו» — and the contains-rule preferred it over the
correct hit sitting at all[0]. So two defects: the city was never consulted at
all, and the fallback meant to repair fuzzy street spellings could override a
correct answer whenever that answer came back in the short form.

geo.ts now reads street and city out of the long id when it is there and out of
the result text when it is not, derives the typed city from what follows the
house number (Home.tsx joins the three fields as street-number-city), and lets
it constrain the choice: if any candidate sits in the typed city, only those
candidates are eligible. Inside that pool the old order is untouched. If no
candidate is in the typed city the old rule runs on the whole list, so an
address the registry spells differently still resolves instead of vanishing.
The containment rule is the same one-sided one research.ts uses on the client,
so «תל אביב» against «תל אביב-יפו» stays quiet. engine.ts and index.ts are
unchanged — the hint is derived, and the new parameter is optional.

scripts/qa/smel-geocode-city.mjs imports the shipped functions rather than
re-implementing them, and checks three things per case, because the city on the
pin is only half the claim: the matched city, the locality_code resolved the way
engine.ts resolves it (every number in the report is keyed by it), and that the
short id form still carries coordinates — it had never been selected before.
