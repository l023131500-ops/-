gannenet (40): twelve lessons carried their whole meta line where the month goes

/library builds its month filter from the mashlima `month` field as it stands,
and on 12 of the 47 lessons that field holds the entire meta line
("אלול  |  גיל 3–6  |  משך: כ־45 דק׳  |  תחום: חברה והתנהגות") rather than the
month. The dropdown therefore offered 19 options where eleven months exist,
twelve of them long strings clipped inside the select; twelve cards printed
that line as their month chip; and picking אלול returned 4 of the 6 Elul
lessons -- the other two reachable only through the garbage entry each had
become. Every lesson was reachable through some option (the counts summed to
52); none through the name of its month.

One monthOf() now serves both audiences -- cut at the first | or ·, drop a
leading חודש -- covering the two shapes the data has: a mashlima `month` field,
and the meta line that is the only month a regular lesson carries
("חודש תשרי · יום א׳ מתוך 5  |  …"). That path had its own
meta.split("·")[0].replace("חודש",""), correct against today's five rows and
breakable the same way by one meta line written with | instead. allMonths()
reads through it too.

No content file was touched: the data stands, only the reading of it changed.
Measured in the browser both ways -- 19 -> 11 options in year order, 12 -> 0
over-long chips, אלול 4 -> 6, and the eleven month counts still sum to 52, so
nothing was dropped or double-counted. tsc --noEmit 0, 0 console errors.
Evidence in QA/gannenet/library-month-0811/.
