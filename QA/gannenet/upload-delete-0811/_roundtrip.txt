"file after delete: 200" is measured milliseconds after the object is dropped,
and it is Storage's own CDN, not our route: re-read a minute later the same URL
answers 502 through /api/upload (upstream gone) while a direct hit on
supabase.co was still 200 from the edge. That short window is what the route's
`s-maxage=300` now bounds; before this step it was `s-maxage=86400`.

The three "DELETE leftover … 404" lines are 404 because the FIRST run of this
harness had already removed them, through this same route:
