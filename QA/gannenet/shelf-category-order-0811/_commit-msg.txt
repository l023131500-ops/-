gannenet (40): the shelf named 21 categories and could sort or file only 11

CATEGORY_ORDER in lib/catalog.ts is read twice. /shelf orders its category
chips by it, under a heading that promises the archive is "מסודרים לפי סדר
השנה". /shelf/upload *is* it - the category dropdown on the add-material
form is CATEGORY_ORDER.map(). The list held 11 entries. The shelf, once the
Drive catalog is merged with the 258 seed files the way the page merges
them, holds 21.

So 937 of 2,977 files - 31.5% - sat in ten categories that all rank equal
and therefore tie. The chip tail came out ordered by the date of each
category's oldest file: ספירת העומר / ל"ג בעומר, a holiday that falls
between פסח and שבועות, was fourteenth, after דפי צביעה. And a teacher
adding her own material could file it into eleven categories and none of
the other ten - including כללי, which is where 679 of the files already
are.

The list now carries all 21, holidays in the order the school year meets
them, then the year-round groups: the weekly parasha, the four skills
tracks, מידות ורגש, the material types, and כללי last as the catch-all.
sortItems and orderedCategories tie-break equal ranks by name, so a
category arriving later from the Drive catalog groups together instead of
scattering through the tail by date.

Verified by rendering, not by reading: next dev with APP_BASE_PATH=/gannenet,
the chip row on /shelf comes back as 21 categories in exactly the computed
order next to the counter "2,977 פריטים · 21 קטגוריות", and the dropdown on
/shelf/upload offers 21 options. Zero console errors. Before and after
readings of the merged catalog, and both screenshots, are in
QA/gannenet/shelf-category-order-0811/.
