40 gannenet: "hidden" removed the material from every screen except the one they all link at

The open line from the last beat was that the "מוסתר מהמדף" chip on "החומרים
שלי" had never been seen on a real item. It has now — one upload by
test@more30.com, hidden through /api/admin/override, photographed live, then
unhidden and deleted (QA/gannenet/hide-roundtrip-0812/).

The chip works. The screenshot is what found the bug: the hidden card offered
two buttons that disagreed. "פתיחה וצפייה" reached "החומר לא נמצא", and
"הורדה" returned 200 image/png 2441B. The admin screen says הסתרת קובץ
"מסירה אותו מהאתר לגמרי"; the shelf list, /api/catalog and /shelf/[id] all
honour that, and /api/upload/[name] — the URL those three link at — consulted
nothing. Anyone holding the address from before the hide (a shared link, browser
history, the service worker's own offline copy) still had the file, which is the
thing a teacher hides a material to stop.

- lib/overrides.ts: isHiddenFile(id) — the single-file question, without the
  LIST + N GETs of readOverrides(). Per-file object wins, empty means cleared,
  legacy map only when no object exists. Not cached: a TTL here is a window in
  which a moderated file keeps being served.
- /api/upload/[name]: GET and HEAD answer 404 for a hidden file. 404 and not
  403, so the service worker drops its cached copy — hiding now evicts the file
  from browsers that already took it. Measured on production: GET 404, HEAD 404.
- /shelf/mine: a hidden card says what happened to the material instead of
  offering two buttons that do nothing.

Open line, measured and written up but not fixed here: unhiding does not come
back. The override object reads {} straight after the write, and both the public
shelf and "החומרים שלי" still saw hidden — readOverrides() reads the per-file
objects without the cache-buster setOverride() uses, and lib/overrides.ts
already documents that Supabase's CDN was seen serving a superseded body. So
hiding lands instantly and unhiding lags, while the admin screen reports "נשמר".
The fix gives up edge caching on every public catalog read and deserves its own
step with a measurement.
