gannenet (40): 54 lessons said which of five meetings they were, in a unit of twelve

The line above every רגילה lesson title printed its position in the unit
as "מפגש {day} מתוך 5", with the total fixed in the JSX. Counted off
content/regular.json, the units are not all five long: the 13 holiday and
value units are (65 lessons), but the five פרשות השבוע books hold 12, 11,
10, 10 and 11 -- one lesson per parasha. So 54 of the 119 carried a false
total, and 34 of those contradicted themselves on their face: פרשת ויחי
read "מפגש 12 מתוך 5" directly above its own meta line, "ספר בראשית ·
פרשה י״ב". The claim was true of the 52-lesson app it was written for;
the 180-lesson package brought the parasha books with it.

lib/content.ts gains unitSizeOf(topic), counted once from regularLessons.
The page prints the total only when it is at least `day`, so a future
package with a gap prints "מפגש 12" rather than a made-up total. The home
page's library card carried the same claim in copy ("5 מפגשים לנושא") and
now describes both shapes.

Verified against every page, not a sample: all 180 prerendered lesson HTML
files were parsed -- 119 carry a unit line, 61 (משלימה) carry none, and
for all 18 units the claimed total equals the number of pages actually in
that unit. In the browser on the production build: פרשת ויחי reads
"מפגש 12 מתוך 12" over "פרשה י״ב", and חנוכה מפגש 2 still reads
"מפגש 2 מתוך 5", unchanged. tsc --noEmit 0, next build 194 pages with 180
/lesson/[id] paths, route JS byte-identical on every route, 0 console
errors. Evidence in QA/gannenet/unit-size-0812/.

The app's own sources are untracked here (/apps/** in .gitignore, "vendored
private source"), so this commit carries STATUS.md and the evidence, as
every gannenet step before it has.
