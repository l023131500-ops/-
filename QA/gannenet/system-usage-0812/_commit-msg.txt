40 gannenet: the system-wide daily cap blocked everyone with nowhere to watch it fill (#189)

#186 set two caps on the worksheet generator and #188 put the personal one on
the teacher's screen. The system-wide cap - 200 pages a day - is the only one
here that blocks someone for what other people used, and there was no screen
anywhere that showed it filling. A teacher who hit it read "the generator
reached the system's daily quota" and nobody, including an admin, could have
known the day was heavy before that.

peekSystemUsage() in lib/rate-limit.ts runs the same single LIST the quota check
already runs: no mark written, no reservation taken, no Anthropic call. From
that one listing it derives the day's total, the number of distinct accounts,
and the per-account breakdown, since the marks are named <userId>__<ts>.

POST /api/admin/usage is gated by the same ADMIN_PASSWORD header as
/api/admin/list and /api/admin/delete, and is force-dynamic for the reason the
quota route is: a cap read from cache shows a stale number exactly when it
matters. /shelf/admin now opens with the panel - used out of cap as a percentage
and a bar, amber from 75%, red once exhausted, plus accounts-today, the personal
cap, the UTC reset, and a collapsible per-account list.

Deliberately not QuotaView with zeros in the personal fields: this screen
belongs to no teacher, and a remainingUser of 20 with no owner is the kind of
number that gets read wrong. A failed count renders nothing plus a retry, not
"0 of 200" - the same distinction the server makes between zero and unavailable.
